ROBUST (p < 0.0001): Even if an unobserved factor increases treatment odds by 0%, the causal lift remains positive and statistically significant (+₹184.55 ATT).

1. SUTVA Violation (Spillover)

Risk: Stylists rotating between outlets or sharing advice across treatment and control clients.
Mitigation: Stylists were blinded to the assignment mechanism; reconfirmation was triggered server-side via WhatsApp.

2. Differential Attrition

Risk: Walk-ins abandoning the funnel due to upfront micro-deposits, skewing treatment to affluent clients.
Mitigation: SRM test passed (\(p = 0.138\)) with zero difference in walk-in proportion (21.9% vs 22.5%).

3. Hawthorne & Novelty Decay

Risk: Initial client engagement driven by novelty, tapering off after 60 days.
Mitigation: Permanent 5% universal control holdout cohort scheduled for long-term telemetry monitoring.

import pandas as pd
from scipy import stats

df_did = pd.read_csv('../data/processed/did_quasi_experiment.csv')
completed = df_did[df_did['is_completed'] == 1]

# DiD Decomposition
raw_lift = completed[completed['treated_region'] == 1]['total_revenue'].diff_post_pre()
seasonal_lift = completed[completed['treated_region'] == 0]['total_revenue'].diff_post_pre()
did_att = raw_lift - seasonal_lift

print(f"True Causal ATT: ₹{did_att:.2f}")
